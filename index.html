Fire your rocket into the sky and dodge birds, balloons, planes, satellites and UFOs on the way up. When you stall, flip nose-down and tractor-beam everything you touch on the way back. Land, fling the whole haul into the air, and shoot it all for cash. Then upgrade and go higher.

Dodge up

Tilt to steer through everything in the sky. Hold the screen to boost. The first thing you hit stalls your rocket, so dodge well and climb high.

Grab down

Flip nose-down and fall back to Earth, tractor-beaming everything you touch. Pop your parachute to slow down and line up the big catches.

Blast it all

Land, fling the whole haul into the air and shoot it for cash before it hits the ground. Shoot everything for a Clean Sweep bonus. Just don't shoot the jellies.

Five skies, five giant bosses

  • Five skies to climb: from sleepy Pinecrest to a sunset island, the northern lights, a neon city at night and the Moon itself, each with its own creatures.
  • A giant boss at the top of every sky: the Sky Whale, the Space Kraken, the Aurora Dragon, the Mothership and the Cosmic Goose. Catch it to open the next sky.
  • Seven guns, from the Pea Pistol to the Laser Gun, each with its own upgrades.
  • Upgrades to go higher: bigger fuel tanks, a wider tractor beam, a ram shield, boosters, parachutes and slow-mo. And paint jobs for your rocket, because looking good matters.
  • Thirty challenges: grab one of everything, clean-sweep a huge haul, climb without touching a thing, and find the secret ones.
  • Tilt or touch steering, and it plays offline.

Free, the whole game

Rocket Haul is free, with no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases. Every sky, boss, gun, upgrade and paint job is yours to earn by playing, and nothing you do is collected.
